How it works

  1. Create your free account. Click Open Dashboard and sign up with your email. Free accounts can track up to 5 keywords.
  2. Track a keyword. Type a keyword, such as yoga mat, and click Track keyword. Track My KW reads the current Target.com results for you.
  3. Mark your products. Add your TCINs in Settings to see your rank and page for every keyword.
  4. Watch it change. Refresh a keyword on different days to build rank history and sharpen the monthly sales estimates.
No installs and no extensions. Your keywords and history are saved to your account and follow you to any browser.
